These are tools which I've put together over the years. Most are available on Bioconda and the rest are installable from their repository; Staphopia is the exception because its [built into Bactopia now](https://bactopia.io/bactopia-pipelines/staphopia)!

Thanks to support from Tim Read and WPHL, I've been able to continue developing [Bactopia](https://bactopia.io/) for more than 5 years now. Bactopia has grown significantly over the years, and there is still much more to come. So, if you try Bactopia, please feel free to reach out with questions or any feedback you may have!

But that also goes for my other tools as well! If you use any of my tools and have feedback or run into a bug, please create an issue on their respective GitHub repository. I will try my best to help in any way that I can.
