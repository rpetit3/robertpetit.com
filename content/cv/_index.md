I am a bioinformatician focused on bacterial pathogens, workflow development, and local inference of open-weight Large Language Models (LLMs). Over the last 15+ years I have developed bioinformatic tools that have been used by thousands of users around the world. I am an active member of the bioinformatic community and contribute my expertise regularly to open-source projects, such as [Bioconda](https://github.com/bioconda/bioconda-recipes/pulls?q=is%3Apr+involves%3Arpetit3+is%3Aclosed). I have developed workflows and skills that use open-weight LLMs and open-source harnesses on local hardware for sensitive data analyses. My expertise has also allowed me to act as the lead bioinformatician for many research projects, as well as receiving funding from multiple awards.
