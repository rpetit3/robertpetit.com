Welcome and thank you for stopping by! I'm Robert Petit (*puh-teet, not pet-it*), and I primarily work as a bioinformatician with the [Wyoming Public Health Laboratory](https://health.wyo.gov/publichealth/lab/) in the great state of Wyoming. While the wind can be pretty annoying in Wyoming, I do enjoy living here and the group at WPHL makes it all the more worthwhile. At WPHL, my role includes building up the bioinformatic capacity, developing new tools to meet our needs, and facilitating bioinformatic training. With the support of WPHL, I have continued to develop [Bactopia](https://bactopia.github.io/), an end-to-end bacterial genome analysis pipeline, more more than 5 years now.

I also act as a part-time bioinformatics consultant for various academic, government, and private organizations. So, if you are interested in collaborating or need assistance with bioinformatics projects, feel free to reach out!
